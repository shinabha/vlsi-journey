# Day 1 — Voltage, Current, Resistance and Ohm's Law

## Date
17 November 2026

## Topics Studied
- Voltage: force that pushes electric charges through a wire (unit: Volt)
- Current: flow of electric charges (unit: Ampere)
- Resistance: opposition to current flow (unit: Ohm)
- Ohm's Law: V = I × R

## Ohm's Law Forms
- V = I × R
- I = V / R
- R = V / I

## Worked Examples
1. V=5V, R=10Ω → I = 0.5A
2. I=2A, R=4Ω → V = 8V
3. V=12V, I=3A → R = 4Ω

## VLSI Connection
Every wire on a chip has resistance. Transistors control current using voltage.
Ohm's Law explains signal delay and power behaviour in chips.
